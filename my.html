Welcome to the Canada Presentation

Canada is a country located in North America. It is the second-largest country in the world by total area.

Geography

Canada has ten provinces and three territories, each with its own unique landscapes and geography.

Major Cities

  • Ottawa (Capital)
  • Toronto
  • Vancouver
  • Montreal
  • Calgary

Culture

Canada is known for its multicultural society and rich heritage.

Languages

The two official languages are English and French.

Conclusion

Canada is a beautiful country with a diverse culture, rich history, and stunning landscapes.
